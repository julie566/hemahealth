Seeing sickle cell disease, not just reading about it

A mobile AR learning app that lets people compare healthy and sickle red blood cells as 3D models, then check what they've learned with a short quiz. Designed in Figma, prototyped in HTML, built for Unity.

Design decisions

Compare, don't lectureHealthy and sickle cells sit side by side, and the difference in shape carries the explanation. Text appears only when someone taps a cell.

AR is optionalEvery AR module has a 2D path, so the content still works on older phones, in poor light, and for people who get motion sickness.

One token set, two platformsThe same colour and type tokens drive the HTML prototypes and the Unity build, so what gets reviewed is what gets shipped.
